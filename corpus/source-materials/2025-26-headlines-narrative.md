Top U.S.National Sports Headlines — Week by Week
(Aug 2025 – Aug 2026)
Two major storylines for roughly each week of the past year, across all major U.S. sports.
Weeks anchored to a specific event/result are marked with the date; connecting weeks
reflect the dominant ongoing storyline of that stretch.
August 2025
Week of Aug 11: MLB pennant races tighten as September call-ups loom · NFL preseason
games begin across the league, with rookie quarterbacks and depth battles in focus.
Week of Aug 18: US Open tennis begins in Flushing Meadows · College football teams open
fall camp with season openers one week away.
Week of Aug 25: US Open tennis heads into the second week · College football’s opening
weekend kicks off the 2025 season (Aug 30).
September 2025
Week of Sep 1: Carlos Alcaraz beats Jannik Sinner to win the US Open (Sept 7), his
second title at Flushing Meadows · Aryna Sabalenka defends her US Open women’s title,
beating Amanda Anisimova.
Week of Sep 8: NFL Week 1 kicks off the 2025 season · College football Week 2 headlines
an early Heisman conversation.
Week of Sep 15: NFL Week 2 storylines take shape · College football rankings shuffle after
upset-filled weekends.
Week of Sep 22: Team Europe wins the Ryder Cup at Bethpage Black, 15–13, holding off
a furious U.S. singles comeback (Sept 28) · NFL Week 4 action continues amid an early-
season Patriots surge behind rookie-year breakout QB Drake Maye.
October 2025
Week of Sep 29: MLB regular season ends and wild-card round begins · WNBA Finals tip
off between the Las Vegas Aces and Phoenix Mercury (Oct 3).
Week of Oct 6: A’ja Wilson and the Las Vegas Aces sweep the Phoenix Mercury 4–0 to
win the 2025 WNBA championship — Las Vegas’s third title in four years (Oct 10) · MLB
Division Series play headlines the baseball calendar.
Week of Oct 13: MLB Championship Series (ALCS/NLCS) determine the World Series
matchup · College football conference races intensify heading into November.
Week of Oct 20: NBA season tips off · World Series Game 1 begins between the Los
Angeles Dodgers and Toronto Blue Jays (Oct 24).
Week of Oct 27: Dodgers become first repeat World Series champions in 25 years,
beating the Blue Jays in a Game 7 thriller decided by an 11th-inning Will Smith homer (Nov
1–2) · Yoshinobu Yamamoto named World Series MVP after three postseason starts on short
rest.
November 2025
Week of Nov 3: NFL trade deadline shakeup: Jets land Sauce Gardner from the Colts,
Cowboys and Patriots make notable defensive/receiver trades (A.J. Brown to New England)
(Nov 4) · NFL MVP race narrows to Patriots QB Drake Maye and Rams QB Matthew Stafford.
Week of Nov 10: NFL Week 10 playoff picture takes shape · College football Playoff
rankings debate the top contenders, including undefeated Indiana and Ohio State.
Week of Nov 17: NFL Week 11–12 storylines continue · College basketball season tips off
with early-season tournaments.
Week of Nov 24: NFL’s Thanksgiving tripleheader draws national attention · College
football’s rivalry week sets the stage for conference championship berths.
December 2025
Week of Dec 1: Conference championship weekend in college football — Indiana completes
a perfect regular season · Big Ten and SEC title games shape the College Football Playoff
field.
Week of Dec 8: Fernando Mendoza (Indiana) wins the Heisman Trophy (Dec 13) after
leading the Hoosiers to an undefeated regular season · NFL Week 15 playoff races heat up
in both conferences.
Week of Dec 15: College Football Playoff bracket is set · NFL Week 16 division races
intensify.
Week of Dec 22: College football bowl season begins · NFL Week 17 playoff seeding battles
continue.
Week of Dec 29: NFL regular season finale (Week 18) decides playoff seeding · College
Football Playoff first-round games are played.
January 2026
Week of Jan 5: Montana State beats Illinois State for the FCS national championship
(Jan 5) · NFL playoffs open with Wild Card Weekend; College Football Playoff quarterfinals
are played.
Week of Jan 12: NFL Divisional Round narrows the Super Bowl field · College Football
Playoff semifinals set the national title matchup.
Week of Jan 19: Indiana beats Miami 27–21 to win the College Football Playoff National
Championship, the Hoosiers’ first title in program history behind Heisman winner Fernando
Mendoza (Jan 19) · NFL Conference Championship games set the Super Bowl matchup:
Patriots beat Broncos, Seahawks beat Rams (Jan 25).
Week of Jan 26: Matthew Stafford named NFL MVP at NFL Honors, edging Drake Maye ·
Super Bowl LX week begins with Seahawks–Patriots buildup in Santa Clara.
February 2026
Week of Feb 2: Winter Olympics open in Milan–Cortina (Feb 6) · Super Bowl LX buildup
continues with Bad Bunny’s halftime show a major storyline.
Week of Feb 9: Seattle Seahawks beat the New England Patriots 29–13 to win Super
Bowl LX, the franchise’s second title (Feb 8) · Winter Olympics underway, with Team USA’s
men’s and women’s hockey teams both advancing toward gold-medal games.
Week of Feb 16: Team USA hockey sweeps gold: both men’s and women’s teams beat
Canada in overtime for Olympic gold · Alysa Liu wins figure skating gold, ending a 20-year
U.S. medal drought in the event.
Week of Feb 23: Winter Olympics close with Team USA setting an all-time record of 12
golds and 33 total medals (Feb 22), second only to Norway · NBA trade deadline reshapes
contenders ahead of the playoff push.
March 2026
Week of Mar 2: NHL playoff races tighten across both conferences · College basketball
conference tournaments begin.
Week of Mar 9: Conference tournament championships crown automatic bids · Selection
Sunday sets the men’s and women’s NCAA Tournament brackets (Mar 15).
Week of Mar 16: March Madness tips off with the First Four and opening rounds · Upsets
and Cinderella runs dominate the men’s and women’s brackets.
Week of Mar 23: Sweet 16 and Elite Eight action sets the Final Four fields in both men’s and
women’s tournaments.
Week of Mar 30: Men’s and women’s Final Four games are played, setting up the national
championship games.
April 2026
Week of Apr 6: Michigan beats UConn 69–63 to win the men’s NCAA basketball
championship, the Wolverines’ first title since 1989 (Apr 6) · UCLA wins the women’s
NCAA basketball championship.
Week of Apr 13: Rory McIlroy wins the Masters for the second year in a row, holding off
Scottie Scheffler by one stroke — the fourth player ever to repeat as champion (Apr 12) ·
NBA playoff seeding races conclude.
Week of Apr 20: NHL Stanley Cup playoffs begin (Apr 18) · NBA playoffs open across both
conferences.
Week of Apr 27: Fernando Mendoza goes No. 1 overall to the Las Vegas Raiders in the
NFL Draft, held in Pittsburgh (Apr 23–25) · NBA and NHL first-round playoff series
continue.
May 2026
Week of May 4: Golden Tempo wins the Kentucky Derby as a 30–1 long shot, with Cherie
DeVaux becoming the first female trainer to win the race (May 2) · NBA and NHL conference
semifinals get underway.
Week of May 11: Napoleon Solo wins the Preakness Stakes after Derby winner Golden
Tempo sits out · PGA Championship week begins in Newtown Square, Pennsylvania.
Week of May 18: Aaron Rai wins the PGA Championship at Aronimink, his first major title
(May 14–17) · NBA and NHL conference finals begin.
Week of May 25: Felix Rosenqvist wins the Indianapolis 500 (May 24) · Knicks and
Spurs, Hurricanes and Golden Knights advance to their respective championship series.
June 2026
Week of Jun 1: Golden Tempo wins the Belmont Stakes at Saratoga, taking two of three
Triple Crown races (Jun 6) · NBA Finals begin between the New York Knicks and San
Antonio Spurs (Jun 3).
Week of Jun 8: New York Knicks win the NBA Finals over the San Antonio Spurs 4–1,
their first title since 1973, with Jalen Brunson named Finals MVP (Jun 13–14) · Carolina
Hurricanes beat the Vegas Golden Knights to win the Stanley Cup, their first title in 20
years (Jun 14).
Week of Jun 15: FIFA World Cup kicks off across the U.S., Mexico, and Canada (Jun 11)
· Wyndham Clark wins the U.S. Open golf championship at Shinnecock Hills (Jun 18–21).
Week of Jun 22: World Cup group stage wraps up and knockout rounds begin, with the
U.S. men’s national team’s run a major storyline.
July 2026
Week of Jun 29: World Cup Round of 16 and quarterfinals narrow the field · MLB All-Star
break arrives at the season’s midpoint.
Week of Jul 6: World Cup semifinals set the final matchup between Spain and Argentina.
Week of Jul 13: Spain beats Argentina 1–0 in extra time to win the FIFA World Cup final
at MetLife Stadium, Ferran Torres scoring the decisive goal (Jul 19) · England beats France
6–4 in a high-scoring bronze-medal match.
Week of Jul 20: MLB pennant races intensify as contenders position for the trade deadline.
Week of Jul 27: MLB trade deadline reshapes contenders ahead of the stretch run (~Jul
31).
August 2026
Week of Aug 3: NFL training camps and preseason games begin across the league · MLB
pennant races head into the season’s final stretch.
Compiled from verified reporting on major U.S. sports events (NFL, MLB, NBA, WNBA, NHL,
college football/basketball, golf majors, tennis majors, horse racing’s Triple Crown, the 2026
Winter Olympics, and the 2026 FIFA World Cup). Weeks without a single marquee event
reflect the dominant ongoing storyline of that period rather than a specific dated headline.
