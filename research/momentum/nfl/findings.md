## Verdict

An NFL hot streak is mostly a good team being seen clearly. Once you know how good a team is, and the betting line does, a streak or a hot November is worth 0 to 2 extra wins per 100 games: indistinguishable from nothing. Champions look hot late because champions are good, barely more than their strength predicts (13 vs 11). Nobody "clicks" in a way you could have seen and the market missed. Caveat: the last 5 include Week 18 rest games.
