COACHING DNA -- do assistants of winners win?  (scripts/coach_tree.py)
Universe: first major-college head job (CFBD/FBS records) beginning 1970-2025.
Outcome: first head job's win% minus the program's win% over the 5 seasons before the hire
(the Issue #185 baseline). Differences are in percentage points. 2 significant digits.
Bootstrap 95% CIs (10,000 resamples); two-sided permutation p (10,000 shuffles); seed 20261029.
Change scores assume a prior-5 slope of 1; the OLS and quintile-match lines do not (fitted slope
is printed), which matters because winners' assistants inherit better programs.

Main sample: high-confidence rows with a boss record, a prior-5 baseline, not interim: n=508
(excluded: 121 high-confidence rows whose prior job was itself a head job or HS/pro-other with no boss record)
