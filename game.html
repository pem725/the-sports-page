Fetching the game…

How to read this card. The market's chance is the betting moneyline with the bookmaker's cut removed. ESPN's chance is ESPN's own model, shown beside it because when the two disagree, that disagreement is worth knowing (ESPN publishes none for hockey). “Where the uncertainty is” lists what could swing this game and that the line may not fully reflect: a team leader who is hurt, a disagreement between the forecasts, a team playing differently lately than all season.

Every halftime check prints how often it has been right, counted on this season's games (…). In hockey the check comes after two periods. The team ahead at the checkpoint wins most of the time. That is the baseline, and any other sign you watch should be judged against it.

Everything here comes live from ESPN's feed each time you open the page, and refreshes every minute during the game. The hit rates come from our own count, with the code in scripts/halftime_baselines.py.
