Fandoma family fever chart — four people, one lifetime of teams, every high and every low

A fan's life is not a won-loss record. It is a handful of moments — the ones that made it worth it, and the ones that still sting. Here are four of us, each plotted from the year we became fans. Joy rises above the line; misery falls below it; the taller the mark, the deeper it cut. Hover any dot for the moment. Watch what the shapes tell you.

How to read it. Every mark is a real, remembered moment — drawn from each fan's own account of their best and worst years. Height is how much it mattered to them, not how the game was scored. The color is the sport. Championships and near-misses sit tallest because that is where a season's whole hope is settled at once.

Why the shapes differ. The same event is joy or misery depending only on what you walked in expecting — so a fan who expects little floats on the joy side, and a fan who demands everything routes all his pain to the one team he can't stop believing in. The chart is the argument: your expectations decide which half of it you live in.
