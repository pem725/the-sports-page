Win, and Notre Dame keeps a two-in-three shot at a bye. Lose, and the bye is gone.

We played out the rest of the season 60,000 times from the committee’s Nov. 24 ranking. Notre Dame makes the 12-team field in essentially all of them, 99 in 100 even with a loss. The top four seeds skip the first round. Notre Dame gets one of them 61 times in 100 now, 65 with a win, and never with a loss.

Why it matters: a bye is one fewer playoff game to survive. And Notre Dame has no conference title game, so this is its last game before Selection Day, Dec. 6.

If you miss it: you will hear that the Irish “control their own destiny.” For the bye, they mostly do not: it also turns on what the teams around them do next week, and our stand-in for the committee is a stand-in, not the committee.

The receiver to watch
1,257receiving yards for Syracuse’s Umari Hatcher in 11 games. Notre Dame allows 159 passing yards a game.

Syracuse has one of the best receivers in the country. Notre Dame has the second-best defense.

Umari Hatcher has 77 catches for 1,257 yards and nine touchdowns. Notre Dame’s defense allows 3.8 yards a play once the schedule is counted, second of 138 major-college teams, and 12.8 points a game.

Why it matters: if Syracuse is going to make this a game, it will be through Hatcher. Syracuse is 4–4 in games decided by eight points or fewer, and it needs this one to be one of those.

If you miss it: a long Hatcher catch early will look like trouble. One big play is not a game plan against this defense; watch what happens on the next series.

The halftime check
82of every 100 college teams ahead at the half won this season (597 of 729). Up by one score: 65 (195 of 298).

At halftime, check the score. A big Irish lead is about as safe as college football gets.

This season the college team ahead at the half has won 82 times in 100, and 65 when the lead is eight points or fewer. The spread is 25.5 points. If Notre Dame leads by three scores at the half, it is over; if Syracuse is within one score, the upset is real.

Why it matters: Notre Dame has outscored its opponents by 25 points a game. Its only loss was close: 28–24 at BYU.

If you miss it: if Syracuse leads at halftime, that is the upset happening, not a fluke of the first half. The live card tracks it: open it on your phone.

Method. The market’s chance is the moneyline with the bookmaker’s cut removed. ESPN’s chance is its FPI forecast on the game page. Ours comes from SP+ ratings (CollegeFootballData, Nov 25): rating gap plus 2.5 points for home field, through a normal curve with a 16.5-point spread. Playoff chances: we played out the rest of the season 60,000 times, starting each team where the committee put it on Nov. 24 and moving it by what each simulated result does to its record against its schedule. That is a stand-in for the committee, not the committee: it knows nothing of head-to-head, injuries or the eye test. The “with a win / with a loss” numbers split those same seasons by this game’s result. Yards per play is adjusted for the schedule (CollegeFootballData box scores through week 13; rank of 138). Series records: James Howell’s historical scores and CollegeFootballData agree on every game. Halftime rates: every major-college game this season through Nov. 21 with a leader at the half.
