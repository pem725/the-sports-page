Before ordering

Embroidery Inc lists a one-time $30 digitizing/setup charge, waived at 12 pieces and on reorders. Require real stitched proofs of both color treatments before production. Tell the digitizer to keep the 1.54 mm halo gap open, use navy thread on white and cream thread on Night Navy, retain the bare domain, and never add a background behind the transparent artwork.

Prices observed September 17, 2026. Confirm current price, shipping, tax, size surcharges, and stock with the vendor.
