1. Choose the shirt

Both finalists are substantial 6.1-ounce ring-spun cotton shirts. Neither feels like a thin promotional giveaway. Ivory or Natural is preferred so the navy artwork prints in one color without a white underbase.

Recommended · Premium

Comfort Colors 1717

Ivory · relaxed fit · 6.1 oz

Soft-washed, garment-dyed cotton with a relaxed, broken-in feel. This is the best choice when the shirt itself should feel like retail merchandise rather than event apparel.

  • 100% ring-spun U.S. cotton
  • Relaxed unisex fit; S–4XL
  • Minimal shrinkage; slight dye-lot variation is normal
  • Front-and-back screen printing offered at the first link

3. Recommended combination

Comfort Colors 1717 in Ivory, with the badge and domain on the front and the newspaper masthead on the back. Print both positions in one navy ink.

Ink and method

One navy ink, approximately PMS 2758 C. Screen print both positions. Confirm the quote includes two print locations.

Proof requirement

Provide a digital proof at actual dimensions before production. Pull all Ivory shirts from one dye lot where stock permits.

Placement

Front art starts about 3.75 inches below the collar. Back art starts about 3.15 inches below the collar.

Small sizes

Reduce both designs to roughly 80% for size Small or youth garments rather than forcing the same oversized film onto every shirt.
