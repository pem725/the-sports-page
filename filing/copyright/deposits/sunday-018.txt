What We Over-Reacted To
Two consecutive Skenes issues (#128 and #129) were defensible editorially — the first graded his current performance, the second built out the methodology — but in retrospect they read as consecutive pitching issues on back-to-back days. The variety rule exists for a reason. A CFB or NHL piece between them would have served the reader better.
What We Under-Reacted To
The Mets rebuild is bigger than a single bullpen-sale piece. Going from a legitimate playoff contender to 51–67 involves decisions that span roster construction, contract strategy, and ownership direction. Issue #132 documented the symptoms. The diagnosis — how a 3.1 ERA bullpen ended up on a last-place team — deserved more space.
