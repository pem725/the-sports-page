How to read it

The tan funnel is luck. If every game were a coin flip, a team’s record would still wander away from .500. After 10 games it could easily sit anywhere from .200 to .800. After 1,000 games it would almost never leave .470 to .530. That shape is the funnel.

Dots outside the funnel are real. 74 programs have played 1,000 or more major games. Only 20 of them sit inside the funnel. The other 54 have been too good or too bad, for too long, to be luck.

Switch the clock. “From season 1” starts every program at once, the way Julian Critchlow’s map of English Channel swims starts every swimmer at hour zero. Yale’s first season was 1872 and Boise State’s was 1996. On this clock they kick off together.
