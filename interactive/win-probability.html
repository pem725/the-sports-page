How Many Points Do You Need to Win?

Every NFL season from 1999 to 2025. The curve is the chance a team won, given only the points it scored. Watch where the coin flip sits.

1999

How to read it. The dark line is the season playing. The two faint lines are 1999 and 2025, fixed, so you can see the drift. The gold marker is the score at which a team is an even bet — a coin flip — and the small tick below it is that season’s average points per team per game.

The thing to watch is that those two very nearly never part company. Score the league average and you are an even bet; score above it and you are a favourite. The curve barely changes shape across twenty-seven years. It slides right, and it slides exactly as far as scoring inflates.

Method. One logistic fit per season on every team-game, 13,928 in all, from nflverse play-by-play. The curve gives the chance of winning having scored exactly that many, which is not the same as scoring at least that many — the second is higher, because it includes every blowout above it. 1999 is as far back as the source reaches. thesportspage.net
