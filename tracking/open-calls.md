# Open calls — public predictions awaiting a grade

Every bold call the paper makes in print goes here the day it is queued, with the
probability our own numbers gave it **at the time of the call**. The Sunday Edition
reads this file; a call that is not written down here is a call nobody will grade.

Grade each one in public when it resolves. Record both halves: whether the pick
hit, and whether the probability was reasonable. A 15% pick that misses is not a
bad forecast; a 15% pick we described as a lock would be.

## Notes

- The October numbers come from a one-off title simulator
  (bracket play-out on `data/nfl-odds.json` and `data/cfb-odds.json`). Its CFP
  field is a stand-in for the committee: fewest losses, then SP+, with no
  conference title games or automatic bids. Treat college figures as ± a few points.
- Re-run the same simulator at each grading point so the trajectory of our own
  belief is on the record, not just the endpoint.
