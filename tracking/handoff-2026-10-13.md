## The standing editorial finding, which should shape the Sunday Edition

The editor's instruction on 2026-10-11: *"I want to learn from this, not keep
score. Keep score of how we do, not relative to one another."*

- **Nobody in the pool beats a coin.** Ten entries, six weeks, not one clears
  ±1.96 SD. Brendan's meanest-mascot heuristic landed on exactly 60.0, the
  guessing expectation.

  edge is undetectable within one season.

  47%, bottom tier 67%, slope −0.44. Simulation shows a ladder carrying no
  information earns nothing in expectation while costing variance.
