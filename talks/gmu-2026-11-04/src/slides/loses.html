How do you serve what people have already decided they hate?

Bring it to the water cooler.

We have been teaching math and statistics and logic to a great many people. The people who could benefit most from it don't learn it. The people who do learn it may or may not use it. So everybody loses. Here is the question for the next half hour: how do you take something people generally abhor, bring it to them, and get them to eat it, like green eggs and ham, before they turn up their noses and decide it isn't for them? I want to talk stats with the people who love it, and teach stats to the people who need it. My answer, and the bottom line of this talk: bring it to the water cooler. Three points, and then your questions.
