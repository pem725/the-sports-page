These slides, and what I said

Take one argument from the room and frame it live: what's the question, what number would answer it, what would a reasonable person expect, and what would they take home. If it's a good one, it becomes an issue, and the person who asked gets their first name in it. Ask them to subscribe, and to forward one issue to the person at their water cooler who will argue with it.
