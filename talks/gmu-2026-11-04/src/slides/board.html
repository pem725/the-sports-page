I want to thank the editorial board. I have an expert in public relations, a physician, and an expert in finance, and I have been teaching statistics for a long time. I do not consider myself an expert. Until I have written about something, run the models myself, and published it, I do not feel like one, and the more you learn, the less expert you feel. That is the first dose of reality anyone doing this needs: a big slice of humble pie. A lot of what I believed about what is right in sport turned out to be old-timey ideas once I dug in. So this is an invitation. You know things I do not. Sports is one avenue, and sports could really benefit from statistics. But statistics are a tool that helps anybody who uses them well, and knowingly, which means knowing the limits of quantifying uncertainty. Bring what you know.
