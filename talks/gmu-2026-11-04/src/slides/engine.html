This is why you need knowledge before you talk to people about a number. It starts with an argument somebody actually has. We score it: is the number ours, is it defensible, is it surprising, does the lesson travel outside sport. The data come only from primary feeds: the MLB Stats API, nflverse, CollegeFootballData, ESPN. A web search can point at a fact; it can never be the source of one, because we once printed a poll that did not exist yet. Then the model, with its limits written down. Then seven checks: reading level, voice, the headline, spelling, layout, and every score in the piece checked against two independent feeds by a script. It publishes itself at 4:30 in the morning, and every Sunday we grade our own forecasts in public. The whole engine, including a list of everything that broke, is open to read.
