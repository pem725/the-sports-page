Wins per 100 · teams after a season of 75% wins or better · 4,269 head-coach seasons, 1969–2024

The story everybody tells: some famous coaches were propped up by their staffs, and when the assistants left, the head coach fell apart. Bowden after Amato and Richt. Dabo after Venables and Elliott. I believed it. Then I applied the statistics to the story. Raided teams do slip the next year; every big season drifts back toward the middle. But compared with teams that had the same big year, raided teams win more over the next three seasons, not fewer. The raid does not make the team good; the team being good brings the raid. I was wrong, and a fan can see why in one picture. Move on; don't teach the model.
