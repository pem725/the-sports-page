“That was the worst class I ever took.”

Every party. One Jonathan.

Start cold, no introduction. I have taught statistics for a long time. Every time it comes up, at a party or in passing, I get the same look: people stare right through me and say, that was the worst class I ever took. Pretty much everybody says it. Then I met Jonathan, who said, oh yeah, me too, I love stats. I said, what? That's unusual. He is the gold dot. And it is not a survey; it's a lifetime of parties.
