Practicing what we preach

We were wrong. In public.

WHAT WE PRINTED

Notre Dame–Navy's halftime score, two different ways in one edition. Both wrong.

WHAT WE DID

Corrected it, then built a script that checks every score against two feeds. It found two more already in the archive.

Correction boxes stay on the page. The failure log is at thesportspage.net/engine.html

If I am going to ask people to update their beliefs, I had better show that we update ours. This past Sunday we printed the Notre Dame–Navy halftime score two different ways in the same edition, and both were wrong. We corrected it, and then built a script that checks every score in a piece against two independent feeds before it can publish. The first sweep of the archive found two more mistakes that had already gone out. Every correction stays on the page in a box. Misses are not embarrassing. Hiding them is.
