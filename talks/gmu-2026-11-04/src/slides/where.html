Take it to the people.

The outpost
the professors

The water cooler
everyone else

Here is the situation, and I describe it without judgment. Fewer people come in, fewer ask questions, and fewer read the text, and the text is essential. I made videos because that was the only way the material was reaching anyone. They are not coming in, so we have to go to them. And I am all in. The outpost is where the professors stand. The water cooler is where everyone else goes to learn about the world: to find somebody to follow, to get an edge, to connect with people, to understand what is going on. Ultimately, to shrink their uncertainty. That is exactly what statistics is for, and they are already looking for it. The usual fixes, more required courses, better textbooks, better teachers, all try to bring the masses to the educators. The alternative is to bring the education to the masses. I am not comparing myself to Gutenberg, but that is the shape of it: put it where people already are, in words they already use. For a big slice of the public, the water cooler is sports.
