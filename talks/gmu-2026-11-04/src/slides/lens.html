This is the logic the whole paper runs on. People view the world through a distorted lens, and the lens perpetuates itself. Whatever the distortion is, fear or disgust or plain hope, it is easy to find supporting evidence, and finding it makes the feeling stronger. So the loop goes around again. The Sports Page takes the emotional state and breaks it down: okay, what is real here? Get a number to it, rather than yelling at each other. Then talk about belief, which is the important part: what did you believe before, and how far should this move you? Then talk about uncertainty: how sure can anyone be? Real case: the Rays were supposed to win 79 games this year, and they won 98. Plenty of people decided early that it was luck and kept finding reasons to think so. The number does not tell you what to feel. It tells you what is settled, so the feeling can go back to the stands where it belongs.
